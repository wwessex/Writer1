    About NovelWriter

    NovelWriter is an offline-first writing app.

    • Chapters are isolated documents (editing one won't change the others).
    • Autosaves locally to your browser.
    • Exports to DOCX / PDF / RTF.

    Advanced planning tools

    • Character, location, and item database: Dedicated panels for biographies, motivations, relationships, and reference images so complex casts stay organised.
    • Plot grid and timeline: A chapter-by-plotline grid to visualise arcs, move scenes, and balance pacing.
    • Corkboard/board view: Optional index-card or Kanban layouts for rearranging chapters and scenes alongside the outline.
    • Character relationship maps: Interactive graphs that highlight connections, alliances, and arcs at a glance.
    • Name generators and templates: Built-in generators for names, places, and prompts plus reusable character, location, and plot templates.

    Collaboration and cloud features

    • Real-time collaboration: Invite co-authors to work together with granular permissions (view, comment, edit).
    • Full version history: A timeline of every revision with diff previews and one-click restores.
    • Continuous cloud sync: Automatic, secure syncing across web, desktop, and mobile with offline editing and eventual sync.
    • Comments and inline feedback: Threaded discussions attached to text ranges for editor and beta-reader feedback.

    Writing quality insights

    • Grammar and spelling suggestions: Optional LanguageTool integration to flag mistakes without leaving the editor.
    • Style and pacing analysis: Spot repeated words, readability scores, and long-sentence pacing issues.

    Productivity and goal tracking

    • Detailed goal management: Expand word-goal settings with adjustable schedules, progress charts, milestone notifications, and motivational messages.
    • Distraction-free focus mode: A toggle that hides the outline and menus so only the editor remains, fading the rest of the interface away.
    • Dictation and read-back: Speech recognition for hands-free drafting plus a “Read to Me” playback option for editing and accessibility.
    • Progress visualisations: Graphs for word-count progress per day, per chapter, and overall, plus metrics like chapter length distribution and character appearances.

    Tip: if the app ever looks out of date, open once with ?nosw=1 then reload.
